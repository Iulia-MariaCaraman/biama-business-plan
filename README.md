# BIAMA S.R.L. – Start-up Managerial & Financial Business Plan

Academic project developed within the Faculty of Cybernetics, Statistics, and Economic Informatics (CSIE) at the Bucharest University of Economic Studies (ASE).

## Overview
A comprehensive business and managerial plan designed for launching a premium medical aesthetics clinic in Bucharest (Dorobanți area). The project covers strategic positioning, human resources planning, operational timeline modeling, and extensive financial budgeting.

## Key Highlights
- **Strategic Management & Market Analysis:** Formulated company vision, mission, and strategic objectives; conducted a comprehensive SWOT analysis evaluating market competition and premium service positioning.
- **Operational Planning:** Structured a 12-month launch timeline using a detailed Gantt chart covering 15 critical phases (licensing, procurement, fit-out, branding, and opening).
- **Organizational Architecture:** Designed a 3-level hierarchical structure, defined job descriptions aligned with national occupational standards, and structured staffing requirements and monthly payroll budgets.
- **Financial Projections & Budgeting:**
  - Total initial investment required: **127,297 RON** (tangible assets, licenses, and operating expenses)
  - Funding structure: 78.5% non-reimbursable grant funding, 21.5% equity contribution
  - Multi-year financial outlook targeting a turnover of **650,000 RON** and sustainable profitability by year three.
